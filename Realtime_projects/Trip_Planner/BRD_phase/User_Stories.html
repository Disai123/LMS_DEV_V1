User Stories & Journey

User stories help us understand the practical application of the requirements from a traveler's perspective, guiding the development of intuitive features.

1 The Weekend Getaway Planner

As a:
Casual Traveler
I want:
To quickly see weather and famous spots in "Coorg" while anchored at "Bangalore".
So that:
I can decide the best travel mode (drive vs flight) and spots to visit this weekend.

2 The Smart Booking Experience

As a:
Busy Professional
I want:
AI to tell me how far a hotel is from the nearest airport before I book.
So that:
I can minimize transit time and maximize my vacation hours.

Success Metric

Completed journey from Source Input → Destination Map → AI Transport Insight → Booking Provider redirection in under 2 minutes.

User Stories

User stories describe the AI agent features from the customer's perspective. Each story follows the format: "As a [user type], I want [goal] so that [benefit]."

👤

US-AI-001: Product Search via Natural Language

As a customer, I want to search for products by describing them in natural language so that I can find what I'm looking for without knowing exact product names or navigating complex filters.

Example Queries:

  • "Show me laptops under $1000"
  • "I need running shoes for men"
  • "What electronics do you have?"
  • "Find wireless headphones"
📦

US-AI-002: Check Stock Availability

As a customer, I want to check if products are in stock by asking the AI so that I know availability before adding items to my cart.

Example Queries:

  • "Is the iPhone 15 in stock?"
  • "Do you have yoga mats available?"
  • "Check stock for electronics category"
🛒

US-AI-003: Add Products to Cart via Chat

As a customer, I want to add products to my cart by telling the AI so that I can build my shopping list through conversation without navigating multiple pages.

Example Queries:

  • "Add a laptop to my cart"
  • "Add 2 yoga mats to cart"
  • "Put the iPhone in my cart"
📋

US-AI-004: View Cart Contents

As a customer, I want to ask the AI what's in my cart so that I can review my items and totals without leaving the chat.

Example Queries:

  • "What's in my cart?"
  • "Show me my cart"
  • "What items do I have?"
🔄

US-AI-005: Update Cart Items

As a customer, I want to update quantities or remove items from my cart via chat so that I can manage my cart without switching to the cart page.

Example Queries:

  • "Change laptop quantity to 2"
  • "Remove the yoga mat from cart"
  • "Update iPhone quantity to 1"
🎯

US-AI-006: Get Product Recommendations

As a customer, I want to ask the AI for product recommendations so that I can discover new products that match my interests.

Example Queries:

  • "What do you recommend?"
  • "Show me similar products to the iPhone"
  • "Recommend electronics for me"
💬

US-AI-007: Multi-Turn Conversations

As a customer, I want to have natural conversations with the AI that remember context so that I can ask follow-up questions without repeating information.

Example Conversation:

User: "Show me laptops"
AI: "Here are some laptops..."
User: "Add the first one to cart"
AI: "Added [laptop name] to your cart"
📚

US-AI-008: Browse Categories

As a customer, I want to explore product categories through conversation so that I can discover products by browsing rather than searching.

Example Queries:

  • "What categories do you have?"
  • "Show me electronics"
  • "What's in the clothing category?"
